Policies & FAQs

Everything you need to know about shopping with Dana Gemz.

Shipping & Delivery

We offer fast and reliable delivery nationwide across Uganda. Once your order is confirmed via WhatsApp, we process it within 24 hours.

  • Kampala: Same-day or next-day delivery available.
  • Upcountry: 2-3 business days via trusted courier services.
  • International: Please contact us directly for global shipping rates.

Returns & Exchanges

Your satisfaction is our priority. We offer a 7-day return policy for unused items in their original packaging. Custom-made jewelry is exempt from returns unless there is a manufacturing defect.

To initiate a return, please send a message to our WhatsApp line with your order details.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Simply click the "Order via WhatsApp" button on any product, and it will automatically open a chat with our team to finalize your purchase.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, Cash on Delivery (within Kampala), and direct bank transfers.

Are your materials authentic?

Yes, we guarantee 100% authenticity on all materials used, from gold plating to natural stones.

Privacy Policy

Dana Gemz Collection respects your privacy. We only use the contact information you provide (phone number, email) to process orders and communicate with you. We never sell your data to third parties.
